OFTC is installed

  1. Finish the steps in the OFTC welcome window. If it did not open, open OFTC from Applications.
  2. Choose Open VPN controls for a compact controls window, or use OFTC's tunnel icon at the top of your screen.
  3. Click Connect when you are ready, then complete browser sign-in if prompted.

Use the same controls to disconnect and see recent VPN traffic. Open OFTC from Applications to bring back its controls window. Terminal is optional.
