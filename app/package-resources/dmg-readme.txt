Open Forticlient (OFTC)
=======================

OFTC connects your Mac to FortiGate IPsec VPNs that use SAML sign-in.

1. Install OFTC
   Quit OFTC and disconnect any VPN first if you already have it.
   Double-click "1 Install OFTC.pkg" and follow the installer.

@@STEP2@@

3. Connect
   Click the OFTC icon in the menu bar and press Connect on a VPN. You sign in
   in your browser with your own account.

The first time, macOS asks you to allow OFTC's system extension in System
Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions.
