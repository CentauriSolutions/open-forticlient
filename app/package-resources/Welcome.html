Your VPN, in the menu bar

OFTC connects to your FortiGate VPN and shows connection status and traffic from a small tunnel icon at the top of your screen.

Updating an existing installation?

Disconnect your VPN before continuing. Installer can ask to close the app. If you cannot find its menu icon, open OFTC from Applications or disconnect in System Settings > VPN. Your saved VPN settings and password stay on this Mac.

After installation

  1. OFTC opens a welcome window. Follow its setup or update button and approve the VPN component if macOS asks.
  2. Choose Open VPN controls for a compact controls window. The tunnel icon in your menu bar opens the same controls.
  3. Click Connect and complete browser sign-in. You can connect and disconnect from the same controls.

Opening OFTC and completing setup do not connect the VPN. You choose when to connect. Open OFTC from Applications whenever you need its controls window.
